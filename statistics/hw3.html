In this homework, I explored the end-to-end cryptographic pipeline that translates a human-readable BIP-39 mnemonic phrase into a secure, checksummed Ethereum address. By dissecting the journey from entropy to the final address, I analyzed the roles of hierarchical key derivation (BIP-32 / BIP-44), elliptic curve mathematics (secp256k1), one-way hashing (Keccak-256), and error-detecting encoding (EIP-55). Furthermore, I placed these milestones in their historical and statistical contexts, verifying why blockchain architecture relies on a specialized suite of diverse cryptographic primitives rather than a single all-purpose algorithm.
